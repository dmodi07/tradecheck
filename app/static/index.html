What you’ll get back

  • Listed
    The name is on a sanctions list. Don’t proceed until it’s resolved.
  • Review
    A similar name is listed. Compare the details to rule it in or out.
  • No match
    Nothing found on the lists checked, as of the date shown.
  • Not checked
    The check couldn’t run. Never treat this as a pass.

What this checks

The US Treasury’s sanctions lists (OFAC) and Canada’s autonomous sanctions list (Special Economic Measures Act and Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act), downloaded directly from each government.

Not yet covered: sanctions Canada applies through the United Nations Act, and the EU, UK and UN lists. It doesn’t cover export permits, tariffs or whether a company is real.

This is a screening aid, not legal advice. A “No match” reflects only the lists and dates shown.
